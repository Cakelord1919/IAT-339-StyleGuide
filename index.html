Interactive Elements

In-text link

CSS selector:

a

Sample code:

<p>This is a paragraph with an <a href="#">in-text link</a> inside it.</p>

Rendered element:

This is a paragraph with an in-text link inside it.

Navigation Link

CSS selector:

.button-nav

Sample code:

<a href="#" class="button-nav">Default Button</a>

Rendered element:

Default Button

Submit/Reset Buttons

CSS selector:

input[type="submit"], input[type="reset"]

Sample code:

<input type="submit" value="Submit">
<input type="reset" value="Reset">

Rendered element:

Text Input Field with Label

CSS selector:

input[type="text"]

Sample code:

<label for="name">Name:</label>
<input type="text" id="name" name="name">

Rendered element:

Radio Button with Label

CSS selector:

input[type="radio"]

Sample code:

<input type="radio" id="html" name="fav_language" value="HTML">
<label for="html">HTML</label>

Rendered element:

Image as a Link

CSS selector:

a img

Sample code:

<a href="#"><img src="https://placehold.co/100x100" alt="Sample description" width="100" height="100"></a>

Rendered element:

    Branding

    What does the company do, or offer?

    • Serves as an independent video game publisher that partners with indie developers to fund, market, and distribute their games globally.
    • Handles publishing operations including digital distribution, marketing campaigns, public relations, localization, porting, and quality assurance.
    • Allows independent development studios to maintain complete intellectual property rights and full creative control over their games.
    • A shopping website that allows gamers to visit and choose from a wide selection of games that our company has helped publish.

    Who is the company to potential customers? (as a person)

    • Think of this company as a trusted business partner and industry advocate who handles the commercial side of games so developers can focus on making them.
    • Professional, developer-friendly, and reliable while maintaining efficient standards for contract fulfillment and product launches.

    Why might this company entice someone to purchase a product or service

    • Unlike traditional publishers that demand IP ownership or heavy creative control, this company prioritizes artist freedom while taking on financial risk.
    • Provides independent studios with the capital, global marketing reach, and platform distribution networks needed to launch games successfully.
    • Provides developers with a streamlined way to reach global audiences and navigate console porting or store placement without managing business logistics alone.
