About Us

We help independent games reach players around the world while their creators keep full creative control.

What we do

GS Studio partners with independent developers to fund, market, and distribute their games globally. We handle digital distribution, public relations, localization, console porting, and quality assurance so studios can focus on making great games.

How we work

Developers retain their intellectual property rights and creative control. We take on the commercial work and financial risk of publishing, offering a reliable partner from planning through launch.

For players

Our store brings together games we have helped publish, making it easy to discover and choose your next adventure.
